Ways to Serve

Find Your Rhythm

From a monthly evening to a full weekend deployment — every role counts.

Deployment Weekends

Full immersion on a live response: travel, lodging, gear, and meals covered. The way most crews form.

Chapter Evenings

A meal, a workshop, and your local crew once a month. Low pressure, high connection.

Remote Desk Shifts

Verify alerts, coordinate routes, and keep families informed — from home, any time zone.

Drills & Scenario Days

Practice the real thing in a safe setting — never too early to sharpen your skills.

Community Outreach

Teach preparedness in schools and workplaces. One talk can save a whole household.

Fundraising & Supplies

Skip the field and still save lives: pack kits, sort donations, and run the campaigns that fund it all.

Volunteer FAQ

Questions Before You Commit

Straight answers, no pressure.

How much time do I need to give?

As little as four hours a month. Most volunteers do one chapter evening plus a weekend deployment a year. There's no minimum quota.

Do I need any experience or gear?

No. Training is free, coordination covers your role-specific gear, and field kits are provided for every deployment.

Is there an age or fitness requirement?

Base roles are open to 16+ and accessible to most people. Physically demanding roles have a voluntary readiness check ahead of deployment.

Can I choose which missions I join?

Yes. You opt in to deployments by area and skill, and you can always decline without explanation.

What support do I get during a deployment?

Travel, lodging, meals, insurance, and a safety brief on site — plus a coordinator who has your back the whole shift.
