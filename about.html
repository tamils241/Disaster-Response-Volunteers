Our Story

Born From a Flooded Street

We started in the Central District after a flash flood cut off 40 families with just hours of warning. There was no dispatch, no coordination — only neighbours wading into a crisis with buckets and bare hands.

That night we built a phone tree on a borrowed laptop. A year later it became an app, a training program, and a network of over 12,000 trained volunteers across five regions.

  • Founded by first responders and local residents
  • Community-owned, volunteer-run operations
  • Every response led by a certified field coordinator
  • Open to everyone — no experience required
Our Values

What Drives Every Crew

Six principles shape how every crew trains, deploys, and rebuilds.

Community First

Local knowledge beats distant planning. We train people who live where disasters happen.

Always Ready

Training, gear checks, and scenario drills run year-round so the first hour is never wasted.

Safety Above All

No heroics, no shortcuts. Every deployment follows certified safety protocols.

Rebuild Together

Recovery outlasts cleanup. We stay connected to families months after an emergency ends.

Everyone Belongs

Emergency readiness fails when it leaves people out. All of our programs are accessible by design.

Grown Locally

Chapters are led by residents, supported by the network — not the other way around.

Our Journey

From One Flooded Street to Five Regions

Milestones that shaped the network.

  1. 2016

    The First Night

    A flash flood strands 40 families. Neighbours organise by phone tree and a borrowed laptop.

  2. 2018

    Five Chapters

    The Central District hub opens and four regional chapters follow, each led by local coordinators.

  3. 2021

    The Response App

    Dispatch goes digital: live maps, skill matching, and silent-alert tools for field crews.

  4. 2026

    12,000+ Strong

    More than 12,000 trained volunteers have completed 4,800+ missions across 132 communities.

How We Work

One Loop, Repeated Everywhere

Prepare. Respond. Rebuild. The cycle that keeps communities resilient.

Prepare

Year-round training, stocked kits, and neighbourhood plans make the first hour of any emergency faster and safer.

Respond

Certified coordinators dispatch trained crews within minutes — search, rescue, shelter, and logistics on the ground.

Rebuild

Recovery outlasts cleanup. We stay for the rebuild, the paperwork, and the long road back to normal.
