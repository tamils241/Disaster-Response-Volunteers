Training Tracks

Pick Your Response Path

Each track leads to a certification and a field role you can grow into.

First Aid & Medical

CPR, wound care, hypothermia, triage basics, and patient handover at the medical line.

  • 2-day core course
  • CPR & AED certified
  • Re-certified yearly

Search & Rescue

Grid searching, marking, light extrication, and safe movement in damaged structures.

  • 3-day field course
  • Confined-space basics
  • Team leader pathway

Logistics & Transport

Supply lines, warehousing, vehicle coordination, and delivering aid where it's needed.

  • 2-day operations course
  • Fleet & inventory tools
  • Deployable with a chapter

Shelter & Relief

Standing up shelters, registration, care for displaced families, and long-term recovery.

  • 2-day shelter course
  • Trauma-aware support
  • Recovery teams

Communications

Radio & digital dispatch, alert verification, and keeping families informed during crises.

  • 2-day comms course
  • Radio licensing support
  • Incident desk shifts

Leadership & Command

For experienced volunteers stepping up as field coordinators and incident leads.

  • By invitation after 3 tracks
  • ICS-style command
  • Mentor placement

Training FAQ

Questions About Learning

Everything you need before you start.

Is training really free?

Yes. Training is funded by donations and partner organisations so cost is never a barrier. Only optional personal kit is at your own expense.

How long does certification last?

Core certifications are valid for one year, with a free refresher course to stay current. Field respondes follow annual recertification.

Do I need physical fitness training?

Base tracks are accessible to most people. Physically demanding roles (rescue, extrication) have a voluntary fitness readiness check before field deployment.

Where do classes happen?

Classes run at chapter hubs across the Central District, plus online theory modules. Register and we'll match you to the closest cohort.
